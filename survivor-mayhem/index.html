Survivor Mayhem Privacy Policy

LowTorch Games · Effective 9 November 2026

Short version: the game itself keeps your progress only on your phone. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads. Purchases are handled by Google Play. LowTorch Games does not run its own servers and never sells your data.

What the game stores on your phone

Your progress (scrap, heroes, upgrades, goals, best runs, settings and an in-progress run) is saved on your device only. It is not linked to your name or an account, and LowTorch Games never receives it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads: optional reward ads you choose to watch (for example to revive or double your scrap) and an occasional ad between runs, never during play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show, measure and protect ads from fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address (used for approximate location),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app version),
  • ad interactions and diagnostic data.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EU, UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent before ads are personalized. You can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Buying Remove Ads or the Supporter Pack turns off the ads between runs.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store) under their own privacy policies. LowTorch Games never sees your payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of what you bought so it can deliver it, and remembers it on your device.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or player profiles.
  • No analytics or tracking services of our own.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • We never sell your data.

The app may vibrate your phone briefly when your character is hit. This uses a device feature only.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: lowtorchgames.support@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days.
