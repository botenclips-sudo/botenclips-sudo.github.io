Survivor Mayhem Privacy Policy

LowTorch Games · Effective 9 November 2026

Short version: Survivor Mayhem does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. Your progress stays on your own device.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress on your device only: scrap balance, unlocked heroes, Workshop upgrades, best run, and whether sound is on. This save never leaves your device and is not linked to your name, account or any identifier. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or player profiles.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo access.
  • The game makes no network requests while you play. It works fully offline.

The app may vibrate your phone briefly when your character is hit. This uses a device feature only and collects nothing.

Purchases

If the game offers an in-app purchase, the payment is handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own privacy policies. LowTorch Games never receives your payment details. If this changes, for example by adding a purchase service that receives purchase records, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever starts collecting data (for example ads or analytics), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: lowtorchgames.support@gmail.com. I reply within 30 days.
