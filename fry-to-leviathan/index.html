Fry to Leviathan Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Fry to Leviathan ("the game") is made and published by LowTorch games ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game collects, why, and who it is shared with. I don't sell your data and I collect as little as I can.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress is saved in the app's local storage on your device: DNA, upgrades, unlocked skins, best scores, your daily best, the path of your best run (used for your own ghost), sound settings and simple play counts (such as number of runs and days played). It is not sent to me unless described below. Deleting the app deletes this data.

The game also records a short video clip of your last moments of each run. The clip stays on your device. It is only shared or saved if you tap "Share clip" or "Save clip", and then it goes wherever you choose to send it.

2. Anonymous player ID

The first time you play, the game creates a random ID on your device. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or contacts. It is used to tie your scores, ghost runs, analytics events and purchases together.

3. Leaderboard and ghosts

Online leaderboards may not be switched on in every version of the game. When they are on and the game is online, it sends your anonymous player ID, your scores, your daily best for the Daily Trench, the form you reached and a recording of your best run's path and size over time (a "ghost run") to the game's leaderboard service. Other players can see your score and swim against your ghost. The leaderboard doesn't show your real name. A leaderboard entry is kept as long as the leaderboard runs, or until you ask me to delete it.

4. Anonymous gameplay analytics

Analytics may not be switched on in every version of the game. When they are on, to balance the game, it sends anonymous events to my analytics service: when a session starts, when a run ends (how long it lasted, the score, the form you reached, what ended it, which mutations you picked, and whether it was a Daily Trench run), when you share or save a clip, when you unlock a skin, and when you finish watching a rewarded ad. Each event carries the anonymous player ID, a random session ID, the date and your platform (iOS or Android). I don't use this data for advertising and I don't combine it with other data about you. I keep it for up to 24 months, then delete it or keep only totals.

5. Optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a video ad to revive or to double the DNA from a run. These ads are served by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address and device information to show and measure ads, and to personalise them if you allow it.

  • On iPhone, the game asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, Google cannot use your IDFA.
  • In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ads, and you can choose whether ads are personalised.
  • You can reset or turn off your advertising ID in your device settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

6. In-app purchases (Apple, Google and RevenueCat)

Skins can be bought in the app. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). I never see your payment card or billing details. Purchases are managed through RevenueCat, which receives your anonymous player ID and the purchase record (which item, when, and the store receipt) so purchases can be checked and restored.

Learn more: RevenueCat Privacy Policy, Apple Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy.

7. What I don't do

  • I don't sell your personal data, and I don't share it for money.
  • I don't ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or microphone.
  • There is no chat and no account to create.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has sent data through the game, contact me and I will delete it.

9. Your choices and rights

  • You don't have to watch ads or buy anything to play.
  • You can ask me to see or delete the leaderboard and analytics data tied to your anonymous player ID. Because I don't know who you are, I may ask you for details such as your score and the day you played, to find your data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have further rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to your data protection authority.

10. Security and where data is stored

Data sent from the game travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The leaderboard, analytics and partner services may store data on servers outside your country.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I will update the effective date at the top. If the change is significant, I will also mention it in the app's release notes.

12. Contact

Questions or requests: lowtorchgames.support@gmail.com. To delete your data, email me with the subject "Fry to Leviathan data request". I reply within 30 days.
